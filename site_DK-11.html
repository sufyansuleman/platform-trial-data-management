This site against all sites

Compared at the same stage of participation, not on a calendar axis. A site in its third month is compared against every other site’s third month. Comparing a young site against an established one on calendar time penalises it for being young, which helps nobody.

Primary endpoint completeness

Days alive without life support at 30 days is the outcome every adaptive analysis reads. A day with no daily record counts as unknown, not as a day free of life support, so missing records understate the endpoint rather than inflating it.
